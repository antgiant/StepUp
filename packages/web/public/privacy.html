Privacy policy

Step Up Helper · Last updated October 10, 2026

The short version: you choose where your data is kept: your own Microsoft OneDrive, or only on your device. Only that storage and the people you share it with can see your data. We run no servers, so we never receive it. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Who we are

Step Up Helper is a free tool that helps families keep track of Step Up For Students reimbursements. It is not affiliated with Step Up For Students.

What the app handles

Whatever you put into it:

  • Student names.
  • Purchases: vendor, date, amount, category, notes, and reimbursement status.
  • Receipts and other documents you add (PDFs and photos).

If you sign in with Microsoft, the app also shows your Microsoft account name so you can see who is signed in.

Where your data is kept

When you first open the app you choose where your data lives. Who can see it depends on that choice:

  • Microsoft OneDrive. Your records and documents are saved in the OneDrive folder you choose, so Microsoft stores them and can see them, under Microsoft’s own privacy terms. Anyone you share the folder with can see them too. The app also keeps one small settings file in its own private OneDrive folder so it remembers which folder you picked.
  • Local only (your device). Your records stay inside this browser on this device. They are not sent anywhere, and no one else can see them: not Microsoft, and not us. Clearing the site’s data in your browser erases them, so export your data if you want a backup.

Either way, the app keeps temporary copies of recent records and receipts you opened in your browser’s storage, so it is fast and works offline. With Microsoft OneDrive, signing out removes them.

Who can see your data

Only the storage you chose (see above) and anyone you share it with. We cannot see it. The app talks directly between your browser and your chosen storage; nothing passes through us. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than showing it to you.

One more thing to know: the app itself is delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub can see ordinary web request details (such as your IP address and browser type) when the page loads. That never includes your records.

Microsoft permission

If you choose Microsoft OneDrive, signing in asks Microsoft for permission to read and write files in your OneDrive. That permission is broad because Microsoft grants it for your OneDrive in general. The app only opens the folder you choose, the folders inside it, and its own settings folder. It looks at other folders only while you are choosing one. We never see your Microsoft password; you enter it on Microsoft’s own sign-in page.

Children’s information

Your records may include children’s names. We do not collect, see, or use them; they stay in your own storage. The app is for parents and guardians, not for children.

Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies or any analytics or advertising tools. It saves settings (like your chosen storage and folder) in your browser’s storage. Microsoft’s sign-in page may use its own cookies.

Optional sharing

If you press Share them on GitHub for category fixes, the app opens a prefilled GitHub page containing only category names. Nothing is sent until you submit it on GitHub yourself.

Your choices

  • Sign out to remove the temporary copies from this device.
  • Disconnect to stop using a folder. Your files stay in OneDrive.
  • Delete your data by deleting the folder in OneDrive, or, for Local only, by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • Remove the app’s access to your OneDrive any time in your Microsoft account’s app permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
